# Demo video script (under 3 minutes)

Record at 1440×900, browser zoom 100%, dark room lighting off the screen. Have the live site open with the Telegram job sample ready, and the Canonical example open in a second tab. Say it in your own words; the lines below are a guide, not a script to read.

**0:00–0:15 · The problem**
Landing page, scroll slowly through the hero while the card gets scanned.
> "Reported job-scam losses hit 501 million dollars in 2024, and most of these scams are checkable. The domain was registered last week, the company says it never charges fees, the same message is already on a scam forum. Nobody has time to dig at 11pm when the offer expires tomorrow."

**0:15–0:30 · Start a check**
Click "Check an offer", pick the Telegram job sample, Investigate.
> "So you paste the message. ProofBeforePay runs a real investigation you can watch."

**0:30–1:15 · Watch it work**
Let the live board run. Point at the step tracker, the signals appearing, claims flipping, the Ultra reasoning stream, and the routing panel.
> "Nemotron Nano reads the message and pulls out every claim. Tavily searches the web, crawls the company's careers pages and opens the link in the message so you don't have to. Nano checks each claim, and anything it's unsure about goes to Super with a fresh search. Then Ultra, once, weighs everything, and you can see it reasoning."

**1:15–1:45 · The verdict and the challenge**
The report appears. Show the Stop, then scroll to "We tried to prove this wrong".
> "Then a second model, Nemotron Super, tries to prove the verdict wrong with its own searches. Here it couldn't, and it tells you exactly what evidence would change the answer."

**1:45–2:15 · The Proof Trail**
Scroll back up to the Proof Trail and click amazon.jobs.
> "Every conclusion traces back to evidence. Click Amazon's own page: it contradicts the fee claim, which feeds two findings, which lead to Stop."

**2:15–2:35 · It says yes too**
Switch to the Canonical tab.
> "And it's not a scam detector that says no to everything. A genuine recruiter email gets a Proceed, and the challenger still tells you the one thing worth checking."

**2:35–2:55 · Cost and close**
Point at the routing panel's savings figure.
> "Each check costs about two cents on Nebius Token Factory, around 60% less than sending everything to Ultra. It's open source, and the evidence is always one click away. ProofBeforePay: check the offer before it costs you."

Say "NVIDIA Nemotron" and "Nebius Token Factory" out loud at least once; judges listen for them.
